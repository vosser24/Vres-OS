**C2 — Migration authority.** Enforce the approved migration cohort inside the migrator, including unapplied identities, not only in installer/release-input validation. D1 remains conditional; no final migration numbering, cohort, SQL or implementation adopted.

**C3 — Database identity.** Require independently enrolled target identity, purpose, effective role and isolation evidence. Bind verification to the actual executing connection; equivalent target binding for separate backup/restore clients. No reliance on database identity fields alone. Cloned or ambiguous targets remain in scope: when identity cannot be distinguished sufficiently for the operation, refuse. Do not assume a port, data directory, ledger hash or read-only intent proves isolation. No live enrollment/contact authorized.

**C4 — Native management commands.** Session-less management invocations are a distinct potential authorization class, not inherently safe or an automatic exception to the host hold. Account for all four original installer invocation sites, including the switch. Preserve original installer/updater transaction; at future authorized design/implementation establish appropriate non-executing evidence and applicable separately scoped clearance/authorization, or use an equivalent preparation/validation approach within the same product without relying on the held host. Vendor documentation and static file evidence do not prove effective executable/policy/registration, absence of session behavior or complete route isolation. No management invocation, service action, hot patch or manifest rewrite authorized.

**C5 — Original functionality.** Preserve cancellation and reopening with fresh task/session authority, atomic effects and immutable history; preserve durable project/source trust, explicit revocation, independent alternate support and governed learning. The present terminal safety hold is not a contradiction with eventual original functionality acceptance. No dormant writer activation now; no permanent functionality reduction or arbitrary deferral of trust invalidation solely because an earlier snapshot had zero events. **C6 — Release activation.** Verify a compatible runtime/schema pairing before ordinary native activation. No first ordinary native session before its required schema compatibility is established. No pointer-only rollback as database rollback or assumed destructive restore.
